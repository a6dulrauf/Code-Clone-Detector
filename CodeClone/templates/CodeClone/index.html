How it works

Three steps, one score

Tokenize

Each source file is reduced to a stream of structural tokens — keywords, operators, and operands — stripping away names.

Vectorize

Those tokens become n-gram frequency vectors alongside Halstead software-science metrics.

Score

Cosine similarity compares the two vectors into one number; anything at or above 95% is flagged as a likely clone.
